Description

English is the subject dedicated to the study of the language and culture of English-speaking countries. Throughout the three-year programme we deepened our knowledge of grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension and writing, while also developing communication and speaking skills.

🗣️ Topics covered

Language, culture & communication

  • Grammar: verb tenses, pronouns, adjectives, adverbs
  • Vocabulary: everyday, academic and technical lexicon
  • Reading comprehension: articles, short stories, current-affairs texts
  • Written production: letters, emails, short essays
  • Listening & speaking: dialogues, listening exercises
  • Projects: oral presentations on cultural and contemporary topics
  • Literature: study of English authors and poetry
  • History: from the English Renaissance to the contemporary age
